# Case coverage

OreFlow covers four process questions with three cases each: liberation (`copper_porphyry_soft`, `copper_porphyry_hard`, `gold_free_milling`), classification (`iron_magnetite_fine`, `nickel_laterite`, `phosphate_clay`), flotation (`copper_molybdenum`, `copper_oxide`, `zinc_sulfide`) and integration (`mixed_ore_high_clay`, `low_grade_copper`, `refractory_gold`).

The workbench distinguishes four process families. Nine cases use the rougher proxy. Free-milling gold adds a one-pass gravity branch on classifier underflow and a separate rougher on overflow. Fine magnetite uses a ground-feed magnetic size-window proxy, without classification or flotation; its variants change grind rather than collector dose. Phosphate with clay slimes discards classifier overflow and floats retained underflow. The cases are authored scenarios, **not** plant flowsheets or calibrated design tools. The gravity size window, magnetic capture and assumed magnetic product grade require ore-specific testwork before operational use. All 12 cases have six variants, but a registry record is not proof that a method applies to that circuit; inapplicable entries have null values and explicit status.
