# Deep online research review

Research date: 2026-09-13. This dossier records the theory, educational material, implementation ecosystem and applicability boundary used to choose OreFlow's method stack. URLs are retained so a reviewer can inspect the primary page or paper.

## Teaching and process scope

The [NPTEL Introduction to Mineral Processing course](https://onlinecourses-archive.nptel.ac.in/noc18_ce14/preview) and its [syllabus](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/syllabus_pdf/105105171.pdf) provide the strongest open course spine for particle characterization, comminution, screening, classification, hydrocyclones, flotation and process economics. [NTNU TGB4301](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TGB4301), the [University of Sydney CHNG5004 unit](https://www.sydney.edu.au/units/CHNG5004) and [UWA CHPR4409](https://www.handbooks.uwa.edu.au/unitdetails?code=CHPR4409) support the process-design and computational context. These sources justify an app organised around a circuit rather than an isolated machine-learning chart.

## Classical process models

Comminution was reviewed through [Bond's third theory](https://onemine.org/documents/the-third-theory-of-comminution), the classic energy literature, and [Austin's population-balance introduction](https://doi.org/10.1016/0032-5910(73)80013-7). Contemporary mill-model examples include [Minerals 2017](https://doi.org/10.3390/min7050067) and [Powders 2023](https://doi.org/10.3390/powders3020012). Crusher modelling was checked against [Minerals 2021](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/11/11/1256) and [Minerals 2024](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/14/9/919). OreFlow therefore implements Rittinger, Kick and Bond as comparative laws, a Whiten-style breakage response and a compact population-balance curve, while stating that a full plant model would require calibrated breakage kernels, residence-time and circulating-load data.

Classification research included Plitt-style hydrocyclone cut-size and partition modelling, with [Minerals Engineering 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.minpro.2009.02.004) as a primary anchor and a later open classification study at [UDSM](https://www.tjs.udsm.ac.tz/index.php/tjs/article/view/1099). OreFlow uses a logistic partition and transparent water, density and feed-load corrections. Detailed cyclone geometry, pressure and rheology are intentionally outside the claim.

Flotation research was reviewed through [flotation kinetic model literature](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mineng.2015.04.010), [a 2020 kinetics study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0892687520300492), [a 2025 mineral-engineering study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mineng.2025.109246), and recent process-learning papers such as [Minerals 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mineng.2023.108179). The chosen educational stack compares first-order, Kelsall fast/slow and compressed-exponential responses, then closes a declared one-stage metal balance. It does not claim froth stability, entrainment or plant-scale residence-time fidelity.

## Open computational ecosystem

[MODSIM](https://www.mineraltech.com/MODSIM/) represents a mature commercial flowsheeting ecosystem. [PrOMMiS](https://github.com/prommis/prommis) and its [documentation](https://prommis.readthedocs.io) represent an open process-modelling and optimisation direction. Dyssol's process-simulation literature includes [Powder Technology 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.powtec.2017.01.061) and [SoftwareX 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.softx.2020.100572). A survey of steady-state mineral simulation is available at [IFAC 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ifacol.2019.09.156). The review supports positioning OreFlow as a didactic and research bridge, not as a commercial flowsheet replacement.

## Data and provenance

The primary downloaded reference is [HZDR RODARE record 336](https://rodare.hzdr.de/record/336), DOI [10.14278/rodare.336](https://doi.org/10.14278/rodare.336), licensed CC BY 4.0. It provides constructed particle-mineralogy cases with train and test sheets. The raw workbook is kept local and ignored; the public repo retains a compact aggregate summary, source URL, license and processing status. A GitHub [froth-flotation dataset repository](https://github.com/Hermit-Labs-Pvt-Ltd/FrothFlotationModel) was reviewed as a possible industrial time-series source, but its license and direct redistribution boundary are not sufficiently clear for automatic ingestion; it remains a link-only future source. USGS [mineral resources spatial access](https://www.usgs.gov/tools/mineral-resources-online-spatial-data-access-tool) and [mineral commodity context](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2017/1079/ofr20171079.pdf) were reviewed for contextual metadata, not used as process labels. A global mine-production dataset at [Zenodo 6325109](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6325109) is also contextual and not used as plant recovery truth.

## Machine learning and deployment

The method selection follows the [mineral-processing ML review](https://doi.org/10.3390/min14040331), [mining ML review](https://doi.org/10.3390/min13060788), an applied mining intelligence review at [Artificial Intelligence Review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-023-10500-9) and a [Minerals process-learning study](https://doi.org/10.3390/min11101118). Ridge establishes a baseline, tree ensembles capture nonlinear interactions, a Gaussian-process point predictor tests a smooth nonlinear family, and PyTorch MLP plus autoencoder provide neural surrogate and reconstruction tiers. No predictive intervals or browser OOD detector are calibrated. [scikit-learn leakage guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html) informs the disjoint perturbation evaluation, which is not a held-out ore-family test. [PyTorch CUDA documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/cuda.html) governs device recording. [ONNX Runtime Web](https://onnxruntime.ai/docs/tutorials/web/) is the browser export path. [SciPy least-squares](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.least_squares.html) and [MILP](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.milp.html) were reviewed as future solver options; the current bounded search stays understandable and responsive.

## Applicability verdict

There is a credible *research question* about whether a conservation-aware surrogate can preserve decisions across ore families. The current evidence supports only an authored simulator, its reproducible surrogates, a seeded perturbation study and a proposed protocol. The external particle descriptors are not linked to recovery labels, the circuit is not plant-calibrated, and no live OOD diagnostic is implemented. The manuscript in `manuscript/` is a proposal, not a completed novel result.
