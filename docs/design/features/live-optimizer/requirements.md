# Live optimizer requirements

Status: implemented in 0.07.000 (CAOS_OreFlow #63); the convergence verdict is in `tasks.md`

Scope: issues #53 and #54 (objective weights). The optimizer runs identically in the bake (Python) and in the
browser worker (TypeScript). It is a generalized pattern search with a progressive barrier (Torczon 1997; Audet and
Dennis 2009), and its search step is screened by the exported surrogate under the surrogate management framework
(Booker et al. 1999). Research: the dossier of 2026-09-28 (not published); the sources it verified are cited on [methodology page 12](../../../methodologies/12_optimization.md).

| ID | Requirement | Named gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OP-01 | THE optimizer SHALL maximize `w_r * M / M0 - w_e * E / E0` over the case's decisions (grind target, collector dose, gas velocity; the grind target alone in the magnetic family) inside the Contract 1 bounds. `M` is the recovered primary payable (t/h) and `E` the total specific energy (kWh/t); `M0` and `E0` are their values at the base state; `w_r, w_e >= 0` and `w_r + w_e = 1`. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_01` |
| OP-02 | THE optimizer SHALL poll the incumbent along the positive and negative coordinate directions on a mesh in the unit cube of the decision bounds, starting at the declared mesh size. On a failed poll it halves the mesh; it stops at the declared minimum mesh size or evaluation budget, and the record says which. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_02` |
| OP-03 | THE optimizer SHALL treat the grade, power and water constraints as relaxable, with the violation `h = sum_j max(0, -s_j)^2` over the relative slacks `s_j`, and the contract bounds as unrelaxable. It SHALL keep a feasible and an infeasible incumbent and discard any trial point with `h > h_max`, lowering `h_max` as the progressive barrier requires. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_03` |
| OP-04 | IF no feasible point is found within the budget, THEN THE record SHALL report `status: infeasible` and the least-violating point, never an optimum. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_04` |
| OP-05 | WHERE the surrogate screen is on, THE optimizer's search step SHALL propose candidates from the exported surrogate, and SHALL accept one for engine evaluation only if the guard accepts its state and the Gaussian process's 95% half-width on recovery is at most the declared bound. Every poll point, every accepted candidate and the reported optimum SHALL be engine evaluations. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_05` |
| OP-06 | THE record SHALL keep, per start, the engine evaluations, the candidates screened, the rejections counted by reason (`guard`, `interval`), every proposal with the surrogate's and the engine's values, and the engine evaluations with and without the screen for the same weight and starts (changed during T3: counts, not every rejected candidate; design, Records). | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_06` |
| OP-07 | THE bake SHALL record, for every variant, the optimum over a declared set of weights `w_r in {1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25}`, so the path of the operating point with the weights is a record. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_07` |
| OP-08 | THE TypeScript optimizer SHALL reproduce the Python record's evaluation sequence, incumbents and optimum within 1e-6 relative, for every variant at every recorded weight. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_08` |
| OP-09 | WHEN the user runs the optimizer at a weight in the workbench, THE optimizer's worker SHALL re-run it at the current state. It shows progress, stops when cancelled or superseded (the client terminates the worker), and never runs without a user action. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_09` |
| OP-10 | THE contract SHALL declare the weights control, with its bounds and step, and the same validator SHALL accept or reject it in Python, in the service and in the browser. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_10` |
| OP-11 | THE Methodology, Benchmark and Implementation pages SHALL report the measured saving in engine evaluations and the surrogate's disagreement, from the records, and the page-claim tests SHALL hold every quoted number to them. | `tests/adversarial/test_oreflow_requirements.py::test_adversarial_op_11` |
