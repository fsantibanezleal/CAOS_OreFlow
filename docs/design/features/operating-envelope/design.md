# Design: operating-envelope investigation

The selected case remains the single workbench context. A pure `analyzeEnvelope` function samples a declared rectangular grid around the currently selected operating point. For rougher, gravity-plus-rougher and deslime-plus-rougher families, the axes are grind P80 and collector dose, with three residence levels. For magnetite, the axes are grind P80 and feed rate; flotation variables do not appear. Each point calls `simulateLive`, and retains its inputs, outputs, constraint failures and stress response. Bounds are clipped to the same visible control ranges. The set is a finite conditional sample, not a numerical optimum.

Feasibility is the conjunction of user-set minimum nominal recovery, minimum declared-stress recovery and concentrate grade, and maximum specific energy, water demand and applicable collector dose. The non-dominated set maximizes recovered valuable mass and minimizes total power and collector mass (or magnetic water use). The recommended sampled point is the feasible non-dominated candidate with highest recovered valuable tonnes per hour, breaking ties by lower power. This ranking is explicit, not a hidden monetary score.

Stress evaluates the selected point under declared +10% hardness and ±10% classifier cut (only applicable families). The lowest recovery is labelled *stress minimum*, not P05 or probabilistic uncertainty. Export is a JSON audit record with a schema version and complete candidate rows; it contains no plant observations.

The App receives an `Investigate` view, with a large linked recovery-versus-energy scatter, candidate inspector, constraints, baseline comparison and apply/export controls. The existing projection is retained as an optional explanatory mode. Circuit, response, methods and variant comparison remain reachable. No new top-level route is introduced.

Sources consulted: [pymoo constraint handling](https://www.pymoo.org/constraints/index.html), [pymoo Pareto/MCDM documentation](https://www.pymoo.org/mcdm/index.html), [SciPy bounded differential evolution API](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.differential_evolution.html), and [mineral mass-balance reconciliation research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0892687518301870). The implementation intentionally uses an auditable finite sample rather than implying convergence of an evolutionary solver.
