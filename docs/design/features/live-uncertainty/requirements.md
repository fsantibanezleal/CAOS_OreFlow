# Live uncertainty requirements

Status: implemented in 0.07.000 (CAOS_OreFlow #63); the convergence verdict is in `tasks.md`

Scope: issue #54 (the uncertainty seed and sample count). The Latin hypercube is generated by one pseudorandom
generator, SplitMix64, implemented identically in Python and TypeScript, so the browser reproduces the bake's
design bit for bit and can re-run it with another seed. Research: CAOS_MANAGE
`wip/oreflow/research-2026-09-28-0.07.md` section 2.

| ID | Requirement | Named gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UQ-01 | THE generator SHALL be SplitMix64 (Steele et al. 2014; Vigna's reference `splitmix64.c`), and from seed 1234567 SHALL return 6457827717110365317, 3203168211198807973, 9817491932198370423, 4593380528125082431 and 16408922859458223821 in both languages. | `tests/test_uncertainty.py::test_splitmix64_vector`; `frontend/src/test/splitmix64.test.ts` |
| UQ-02 | THE uniform draw SHALL be `(z >> 11) * 2^-53`, and the two languages SHALL agree bit for bit on the first 10,000 draws of the bake's seed. | `tests/test_uncertainty.py::test_uniform_bits`; `frontend/src/test/splitmix64.test.ts` |
| UQ-03 | THE Latin hypercube of `n` samples in `d` inputs SHALL take, per input in declared order, a Fisher-Yates permutation of the strata and then one uniform per stratum, `u = (pi_j(i) + U) / n`. Every stratum of every input SHALL hold exactly one sample. | `tests/test_uncertainty.py::test_lhs_strata`; `frontend/src/test/lhs.test.ts` |
| UQ-04 | THE bake's uncertainty record SHALL come from this design with the declared default seed and sample count, and SHALL keep the 0.06 fields (quantiles, probabilities, factors, values). | `scripts/check_artifacts.py` uncertainty schema |
| UQ-05 | THE browser SHALL reproduce the bake's factors bit for bit and its quantiles and probabilities within 1e-6 relative, for every nominal state. | `frontend/src/test/uncertainty-parity.test.ts` over the 12 nominal states |
| UQ-06 | WHEN the user sets another seed or sample count in the workbench, THE worker SHALL re-run the design, show progress, stop between samples when cancelled or superseded, and show the result beside the baked record. It SHALL never start without a user action. | `frontend/src/test/worker-uncertainty.test.ts`; `frontend/gate.mjs` uncertainty control check |
| UQ-07 | THE contract SHALL declare the seed (an integer from 0 to 2^53 - 1) and the sample count (32 to 512, in steps of 32) with their messages, and the same validator SHALL accept or reject them everywhere. | `tests/test_contract.py::test_uncertainty_controls_declared`; `frontend/src/test/contract.test.ts` |
| UQ-08 | THE Sobol sensitivity record SHALL stay the SALib Saltelli design (baked only), and the page SHALL say that it is not re-run live. | `frontend/src/test/benchmark-claims.test.ts` uncertainty block |
